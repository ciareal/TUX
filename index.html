SuperTuxKart

WebAssembly port, running in your browser

An experimental build of SuperTuxKart compiled to WebAssembly with Emscripten. The port is by ading2210 (source). Rendering uses OpenGL ES 2, networking is not available, and performance is below native.

Starting up…

Assets are cached in this browser after the first download. Game progress and settings are stored locally and persist between sessions.
